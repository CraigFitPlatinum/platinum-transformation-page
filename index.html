Guided by Someone Who’s Walked the Path

I’m Craig. I grew up in Brixton, struggling with ADHD and dyslexia in a school system that didn’t understand me. Then I discovered movement — and it became my language.

That language took me from street dance on the pavements of South London to Rambert, one of the world’s most prestigious dance institutions. From there, to the West End stage as Dance Captain on The Lion King, Bombay Dreams, Flashdance, and Fame — and to performing live at the Royal Variety Performance in front of the Royal Family.

Along the way, I spent decades studying the traditions beneath movement — Ayurveda, Chinese Medicine, Yoga, Tai Chi, breathwork, meditation, and the emerging sciences of biohacking and longevity. I hold formal qualifications in all of them.
