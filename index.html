Who Craig is

Guided by someone
who walked the path.

Craig James grew up in Brixton, navigating ADHD and dyslexia through a school system that could not see him. He found his voice through movement. From street dance on the pavements of South London to Rambert, one of the world's most prestigious dance institutions.

That path took him to the West End as Dance Captain on The Lion King, Bombay Dreams, Flashdance, and Fame. It took him to the Royal Variety Performance in front of the Royal Family. He was managed by Gielgud Management from 1995 to 2010 alongside some of the most recognised names in British theatre.

Alongside that performance career, Craig spent decades studying the traditions beneath movement: Ayurveda, Chinese Medicine, Tai Chi, breathwork, meditation, and the emerging sciences of biohacking and longevity. He holds formal qualifications in all of them. Total experience: 40+ years, since 1983.
